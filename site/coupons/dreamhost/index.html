Does the first-year hosting price continue at renewal?

The official Yearly Web Hosting Launch card lists $2.89/mo for the first year and automatic renewal at $10.99/mo after one year. Its full term plus taxes is charged at checkout.

What are the main free-domain conditions?

Eligible new accounts receive one credit for one qualifying domain extension's first registration year. It must be used within the first three months after signup. Later renewals are paid, and closing hosting early can make the registration cost due.

Is the Remixer trial the same as the hosting money-back guarantee?

No. Remixer has an advertised 30-day free trial without a credit card. The Terms separately limit the hosting money-back guarantee by service and payment method, and explicitly exclude Remixer and VPS from that guarantee.
