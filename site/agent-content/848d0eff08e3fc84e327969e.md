## Vultr coupon FAQ

### Are there real Vultr coupon codes on this page?

Yes. Vultr's official coupons page currently lists VULTRMATCH, FLYTWOHUNDRED, FLY300VULTR and 250VULTRFLY. The page records these official listings; no paid redemption test was performed.

[Official code listing](https://www.vultr.com/coupons/) · Checked 2026-10-18T17:23+08:00

### How do I get the advertised Vultr credit?

Follow the matching official promotion link and check its conditions. Vultr's FAQ says a valid code can be redeemed in the customer portal's Gift Code section, with a valid credit card or PayPal linked. New-customer and selected-product restrictions apply, with one promotion per user.

[Official promotion restrictions](https://www.vultr.com/coupons/)
[Official redemption FAQ](https://www.vultr.com/resources/faq/#addpromocode) · Checked 2026-10-18T17:23+08:00

### Can I combine the deposit match with the $300 code?

The official coupons page says promotional offers cannot be combined. Do not add the listed credits together or assume an existing account qualifies.

[Official non-stacking restriction](https://www.vultr.com/coupons/) · Checked 2026-10-18T17:23+08:00

### Do all the listed credits last 12 months?

No such shared lifetime was verified. The match page specifically states 12 months after issuance for match credit. The general FAQ says promotional credits expire; the other landing pages checked did not publish their specific credit lifetimes.

[Official match-credit expiry](https://www.vultr.com/match/)
[Official expiry notice](https://www.vultr.com/resources/faq/#addpromocode) · Checked 2026-10-18T17:23+08:00

### Does shutting down a server stop billing?

No. The official FAQ says stopped instances remain billable until destroyed. Use the provider's destruction instructions when you no longer need that virtual machine, and check any other resources separately.

[Official stop-versus-destroy FAQ](https://www.vultr.com/resources/faq/#stoppedinstancecharges) · Checked 2026-10-18T17:23+08:00
