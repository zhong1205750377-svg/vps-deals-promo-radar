## Our quantity-and-operation ledger for the registration coupon

We recorded the coupon category, domain ending, quantity interval, unit price and validity window as separate fields. This worksheet joins the official overview to the detailed registration-coupon table. It is an original document comparison, not a purchased coupon package, a domain registration or a successful checkout. A displayed catalogue amount alone does not establish the amount payable by a particular account.

### Keep an overview amount separate from a purchasable quantity row

The overview displays a lower headline amount than any of the four unit-price rows in the detailed table we reviewed. Our ledger retains both observations instead of silently selecting the cheaper one. It labels the overview as a catalogue display and labels each detailed row with its own quantity interval. The existence of an overview amount is not evidence that a small basket can obtain it.

For a synthetic basket of two registration coupons, the worksheet chooses the first detailed quantity row. It does not select the final bulk row or the overview headline. The calculation multiplies the assumed quantity by that row's unit price. It is a document-based subtotal only: no order was placed, no account eligibility was checked and no tax or payment surcharge was established. Those unknown fields are deliberately left outside the calculation rather than filled with invented zeroes.

### Check the boundaries without turning a bigger basket into a recommendation

Our boundary examples compare a hypothetical quantity of one hundred and ninety-nine with two hundred. The first belongs to the first interval; the second belongs to the next. The unit price changes at the boundary, but the number of units also changes. We compare both resulting subtotals instead of claiming that a lower unit price always produces a smaller payment. Buying more coupons can lower the per-coupon amount while increasing the total commitment.

The same classification rule is tested at five hundred and one thousand. The worksheet uses only the current detailed table, and it does not interpolate an undisplayed price between rows. These amounts are synthetic arithmetic outputs from official inputs, not invoices. We do not recommend purchasing an extra coupon solely to reach a tier, particularly because the official terms make validity and refund restrictions relevant to unused stock.

### Match the operation before assigning the coupon

The overview separates registration, dropcatch, renewal and transfer categories. Our ledger keeps the selected registration coupon in the registration column. A shared domain ending does not make the coupon valid for every operation on that domain. A renewal or transfer requires its own matching official product conditions. We therefore do not reuse this registration table as a renewal quote or treat a transfer deduction coupon as the full price of a transferred domain.

Finally, automatic application describes how the system selects an available coupon at checkout; it does not mean the coupon package is free. The detailed validity period and the reseller commission exclusion stay attached to this specific product. We quote the published timestamps without inventing an expiry timezone that the coupon clause does not specify. The site's separate system clock is not enough to establish that missing detail. Our review date records the observation and does not extend the coupon's life.
