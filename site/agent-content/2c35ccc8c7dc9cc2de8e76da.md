## SiteGround coupon FAQ

### Do I need a SiteGround coupon code?

No. SiteGround's official promo-code article says its eligible discount is already applied automatically and there is no coupon-code field at checkout.

[Official promo-code policy](https://www.siteground.com/kb/siteground-promo-code) · Checked 2026-10-18T15:53+08:00

### How do I get the current SiteGround discount?

Open the official web-hosting plans page and select the plan and billing term. The page checked labels StartUp and GrowBig “Save 83%” and GoGeek “Save 82%”, with 12-month prepayment. Check the separate renewal price before ordering.

[Official US plan promotion](https://www.siteground.com/web-hosting.htm)
[Official signup policy](https://www.siteground.com/kb/siteground-promo-code) · Checked 2026-10-18T15:53+08:00

### Are the introductory prices the renewal prices?

No. The page checked lists StartUp at $2.99/mo renewing at $17.99/mo, GrowBig at $4.99/mo renewing at $29.99/mo, and GoGeek at $7.99/mo renewing at $44.99/mo. Each displayed plan is prepaid for 12 months.

[Official US plan prices](https://www.siteground.com/web-hosting.htm) · Checked 2026-10-18T15:53+08:00

### Is the money-back guarantee a free hosting trial?

No. SiteGround's official article says there are no free trial hosting accounts. Shared hosting has a 30-day money-back guarantee, subject to its refund conditions and exclusions.

[Official hosting-trial policy](https://www.siteground.com/kb/offer-free-trial)
[Official refund policy](https://www.siteground.com/kb/do_i_get_a_refund) · Checked 2026-10-18T15:53+08:00

### What happens to a free domain if I cancel hosting?

The refund policy says domain fees are non-refundable and may be due on cancellation even if initially waived as part of a promotion. Read the domain and refund terms before buying.

[Official domain-fee exclusion](https://www.siteground.com/kb/do_i_get_a_refund) · Checked 2026-10-18T15:53+08:00
