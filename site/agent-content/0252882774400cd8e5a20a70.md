## DigitalOcean coupon FAQ

### Is there a verified DigitalOcean coupon code on this page?

No reusable code was found on the official pages checked. This page does not publish an unverified code. The confirmed offer is the $5 Managed Agents free-trial credit for new DigitalOcean customers.

[Official Managed Agents trial](https://www.digitalocean.com/products/managed-agents) · Checked 2026-10-18T15:14+08:00

### How do I get the $5 free-trial credit?

Open the official Managed Agents page and follow Start trial. Its footnote limits the trial to new DigitalOcean customers. Existing customers can start on a paid plan.

[Official Managed Agents trial](https://www.digitalocean.com/products/managed-agents) · Checked 2026-10-18T15:14+08:00

### Does this mean every Droplet receives a $5 discount?

No. The verified trial offer is displayed under Managed Agents. The general pricing page separately lists Droplets starting at $4/month; this page does not claim that the Managed Agents trial is a universal Droplet coupon.

[Official trial scope](https://www.digitalocean.com/products/managed-agents)
[Official pricing](https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing) · Checked 2026-10-18T15:14+08:00

### When does the Managed Agents trial credit expire?

The Managed Agents trial section checked does not publish an expiry date or duration. The general pricing FAQ gives a 90-day credit example; that example is not evidence of this trial's validity period.

[Official trial scope](https://www.digitalocean.com/products/managed-agents)
[Official credit FAQ](https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing) · Checked 2026-10-18T15:14+08:00

### Does powering off a bundled CPU Droplet stop its charges?

No. Official billing documentation says powered-off bundled CPU Droplets remain billable and must be destroyed to end Droplet billing. Associated snapshots, volumes and volume snapshots are not destroyed by default.

[Official billing](https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/droplets/details/pricing/)
