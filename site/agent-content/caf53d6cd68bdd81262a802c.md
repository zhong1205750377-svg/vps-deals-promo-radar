## DreamHost coupon FAQ

### Is there a verified standalone DreamHost coupon code here?

No standalone code for manual entry was verified on the official pages checked. The hosting page supplies its own promotional Sign Up Now links; use those links to reach the advertised plan. This page does not present a link parameter as a tested reusable coupon.

[Official hosting offer links](https://www.dreamhost.com/hosting/) · Checked 2026-10-18T16:04+08:00

### Is SAVE 75% an official label or your calculation?

It is the exact savings label displayed for Web Hosting Launch with Yearly selected on the official page checked. We did not calculate a savings percentage from the first-year and renewal prices.

[Official printed promotion label](https://www.dreamhost.com/hosting/) · Checked 2026-10-18T16:04+08:00

### Does the first-year hosting price continue at renewal?

The official Yearly Web Hosting Launch card lists $2.89/mo for the first year and automatic renewal at $10.99/mo after one year. Its full term plus taxes is charged at checkout.

[Official pricing and renewal notice](https://www.dreamhost.com/hosting/) · Checked 2026-10-18T16:04+08:00

### What are the main free-domain conditions?

Eligible new accounts receive one credit for one qualifying domain extension's first registration year. It must be used within the first three months after signup. Later renewals are paid, and closing hosting early can make the registration cost due.

[Official free-domain program](https://help.dreamhost.com/hc/en-us/articles/215249528-Free-domain-registration-program) · Checked 2026-10-18T16:04+08:00

### Is the Remixer trial the same as the hosting money-back guarantee?

No. Remixer has an advertised 30-day free trial without a credit card. The Terms separately limit the hosting money-back guarantee by service and payment method, and explicitly exclude Remixer and VPS from that guarantee.

[Official Remixer trial](https://www.dreamhost.com/remixer-website-builder/)
