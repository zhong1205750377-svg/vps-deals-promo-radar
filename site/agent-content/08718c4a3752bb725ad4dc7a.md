## Three offer checks before selecting a domain

We checked the public promotion cards and built the following transaction checks from their fields. These are reproducible document checks, not a paid order or coupon-redemption test.

- Tier-price check: locate the transaction label first, then read the card price and the qualification note independently. For .COM, the displayed $8.75 is not the same claim as the separate $8.00 minimum for Tiers 2–6. The difference is calculated as $8.75 − $8.00 = $0.75; it is a comparison between two stated eligibility levels, not an additional discount available to everyone. A buyer with no confirmed tier should not substitute the lower number.
- Expiry check: bind each end date to the exact suffix and transaction. The .LIVE first-year standard registration ends December 3, 2026, while the .ART first-year standard registration ends December 31, 2026. A date copied from a neighbouring card would change the represented offer. Our table therefore carries a separate end-date field for every row instead of presenting a single site-wide expiration.
- Premium-exception check: start with the general premium-domain exclusion, then check the chosen card for an explicit exception. The .ART card separately advertises 75% off premium registrations. This does not prove that a premium .COM, a renewal or a transfer receives that percentage. In the table, the premium statement stays attached to .ART rather than becoming a store-wide discount headline.

### A transaction is part of the price

For the displayed .COM cards, transfer minus registration is $10.80 − $8.75 = $2.05. For .NET, it is $11.35 − $8.75 = $2.60. Those calculations compare different transaction types; they are not savings claims and do not imply that transferring is a substitute for registering an unavailable name. To reproduce the check, select the suffix, write down REGISTRATION or TRANSFER, copy only the current price and keep the associated eligibility and expiry next to it.

A public card is not a final checkout receipt. These checks do not establish customer-specific eligibility, taxes, successful redemption or future renewal totals. No account was opened, domain purchased or payment submitted for this review. If the final order contradicts a field recorded here, rely on the provider's current order details and ask it to clarify before paying.

## Questions about selecting the promotion

### Is there an official NameSilo promo code to copy?

The direct-coupon section on the official support page currently says there are no active coupons. The official activity page nevertheless lists domain promotions. Use those promotion links; this page does not invent a code.

### Why are there two prices associated with the .COM promotion?

The activity card displays $8.75 and separately describes registrations as low as $8.00 for Discount Program Tiers 2–6. These are not interchangeable eligibility claims. Verify your tier before relying on the lower figure.
