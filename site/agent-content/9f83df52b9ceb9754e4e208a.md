# Did that cheap VPS price change, or was the page just refreshed?

Compare two dated records of the same plan, currency and billing basis, not the date printed on a comparison article. Our saved cheap VPS observations show four Hostinger plans with unchanged recorded prices between October 5 and October 7, 2026. That does not prove the provider kept the price unchanged throughout the interval, and it is not a current checkout quote.

## What our saved records actually show

Our two retained datasets record KVM 1 at USD 6.49, KVM 2 at USD 8.99, KVM 4 at USD 12.99 and KVM 8 at USD 25.99, each labelled monthly in the stored records. The first dataset carries the UTC observation time 2026-10-05T06:47:28Z; the second carries 2026-10-07T00:46:55Z. Each pair points to the same official Hostinger VPS page. We extracted these values from our repository history, rather than reconstructing an earlier price from today’s page. The downloadable ledger preserves the two revision identifiers and input hashes so this comparison can be reproduced.

## A timestamp change is not a price change

For KVM 1 the recorded difference is 6.49 minus 6.49, or USD 0.00. The corresponding subtractions are 8.99 minus 8.99 for KVM 2, 12.99 minus 12.99 for KVM 4 and 25.99 minus 25.99 for KVM 8; every recorded difference is zero. The second observation is 151167 seconds after the first: 41 hours, 59 minutes and 27 seconds. Those inputs establish a refreshed observation timestamp without an observed price-field change in these four matched rows. They do not establish a discount, a price freeze, or a continuous monitoring record. A refreshed timestamp does not produce a percentage saving figure on this page.

## The comparison refuses incompatible rows

Our reproducible comparison joins records using provider, plan title, currency, billing-period label and official source URL. We tested a currency mismatch by changing one input to EUR: it no longer matches the USD row. We also tested a billing-period mismatch by changing month to year: that row is rejected instead of being subtracted. With the unchanged KVM 1 pair, the accepted subtraction returns zero. These are deterministic software checks against saved observations; the mismatch examples are synthetic test fixtures, not additional provider offers. A missing matched row must remain unpaired, rather than becoming a zero-price offer.

## What this evidence cannot establish

The parser records a price and a monthly label; it does not preserve all configuration, commitment, renewal, tax or checkout conditions. A matched key therefore establishes comparability only within the saved fields. A provider could change an unrecorded condition while leaving the price unchanged. We have not ordered these plans, obtained a customer-specific quote, or tested a server. Confirm the current official terms before buying.

## Does an unchanged recorded price mean the cheapest offer is still available?

No. These observations do not establish availability or a market-wide cheapest ranking. Use them as evidence of what this site recorded at two particular times. If you need a buying decision, obtain the current offer and its commitment terms from the provider; do not treat our dated ledger as a guaranteed promotion.
